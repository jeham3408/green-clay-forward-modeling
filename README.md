# Green Clay forward modelling at Nini and Sleipner

Research accompanying Nature’s Plug at the 2026 FORCE VibeCode Hackathon. The Nini well logs constrain how much elastic properties change across the clay. At Sleipner the experiment asks whether a low far-to-near amplitude ratio comes from a softer interval or from water-layer multiples.

Read the [initial assessment](vurdering.md) for the Nini measurements and the hypotheses. The [Sleipner modelling results](sleipner_exp/RESULTAT.md), [stack-by-stack reanalysis](sleipner_ekte_stakkvis_kontroll/NOTAT.md) and [spatial reanalysis](sleipner_ekte_romlig_kontroll/NOTAT.md) update the interpretation. A substantial part of the low ratio comes from increased near-stack energy. A remaining far-stack deficit cannot be uniquely attributed to Green Clay or to low shear velocity.

The assessment and the individual analyses document their sources, processing choices and measurement windows. The repository includes Python analysis scripts, derived tables and figures. Re-running field analyses requires obtaining the underlying surveys from their original providers and setting the data paths for each script. The large SEG-Y volumes are distributed by their providers.
